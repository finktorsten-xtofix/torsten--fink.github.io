Golf Handicap Calculator

Calculates your handicap under the World Handicap System. Your Score Differentials give the Handicap Index, the index gives the Course Handicap for your next round. 9-hole rounds count, and every step of the maths stays visible.

Enter your rounds. The adjusted gross score is on your club's results list, Course Rating and Slope are on the scorecard. Every number can be expanded to show how it was calculated.

Everything is stored in this browser only. No account, no upload. If you start on your laptop, your phone will show an empty list, so there is export and import further down.

There is no course database on purpose. Course Rating and Slope depend on the tee and change with every re-rating, and outdated values would be worse than none. The calculator remembers every course you enter once.

The rules: WHS standard as published by the USGA and The R&A, used by national associations in 135 countries. If your home club is in Germany, switch to the German DGV version under Settings.

Your official Handicap Index is the one your home club keeps. This calculator recalculates it as closely as the published rules allow.

Info What the calculator does and does not do

  • Adjusted gross score, not gross. Enter the adjusted gross score, with every hole capped at net double bogey, which is par plus handicap strokes plus 2. If you enter the raw gross score, your differential will come out too high.
  • 9 holes. The differential for the nine holes played is combined with an expected value for the nine not played. The formula used here is the one the German Golf Association (DGV) publishes in its 2024 handicap brochure. According to the golf press, the WHS works internally with separate models for women and men, so small differences from your club's value are possible. For 9 holes half the PCC counts, as set out in Rule 5.1b.
  • Fewer than 20 scores. WHS standard: a first index after three scores, then the table in Rule 5.2a. The German DGV version issues an index from the first score.
  • Exceptional scores. If a differential is 7.0 or more below the index at the time of play, the calculator subtracts 1.0 or 2.0 from the last 20 differentials, as the WHS does.
  • Soft cap and hard cap. From 20 scores on, any rise of more than 3.0 above your lowest index of the last 365 days is halved. The index never rises more than 5.0 above that lowest value.
  • Not covered. Rounds of 10 to 17 holes, decisions of the handicap committee, local rules of individual national associations other than the German DGV version. Playing Handicap with handicap allowances is in the Stableford calculator (in German).

Formula How to calculate your handicap: formula and example

Every round is first turned into a Score Differential. It shows how many strokes above the Course Rating you played, adjusted for how difficult the course is.

Score Differential = (113 ÷ Slope) × (adjusted gross score − Course Rating − PCC)
Example: adjusted gross score 89, Course Rating 71.2, Slope 128, PCC 0
= (113 ÷ 128) × (89 − 71.2 − 0) = 15.71
rounded: 15.7

The best eight of your last 20 differentials count. Their average is your Handicap Index. With fewer than 20 scores, a table applies. With three scores, for example, only the best one counts, minus 2.0.

9H How to calculate a 9-hole handicap

A 9-hole round is converted to 18 holes. The differential for the nine holes played gets an expected value for the nine not played. That value depends on your Handicap Index at the time of play.

SD nine played = (113 ÷ Slope) × (score − CR − ½ × PCC)
Example: score 45, CR 35.6, Slope 128, PCC 0 = 8.30
SD nine expected = ((HI × 1.04) + 2.4) ÷ 2
Example: HI 18.4 = 10.77
SD 18 = 8.30 + 10.77 = 19.07
rounded: 19.1

Without an existing index the calculator cannot count a 9-hole round. In that case enter your club's index under Settings.

CH From Handicap Index to Course Handicap

The index is the same on every course. How many strokes you receive on a particular course and tee is the Course Handicap.

Course Handicap = HI × (Slope ÷ 113) + (CR − Par)
Example: HI 18.4, Slope 132, CR 72.4, Par 72
= 18.4 × (132 ÷ 113) + 0.4 = 21.89
rounded: 22 strokes

For competitions, the handicap allowance of the format is applied on top. The Stableford calculator (in German) does that.

FAQ Frequently asked questions

Is the result my official handicap?
No. Your official Handicap Index is the one your home club keeps through its national association. The calculator recalculates it from the published Rules of Handicapping. Small differences are possible, especially with 9-hole rounds.

How is a golf handicap calculated?
Every round becomes a Score Differential: (113 ÷ Slope) × (adjusted gross score − Course Rating − PCC). The Handicap Index is the average of the best eight of your last 20 differentials. With fewer than 20 scores a table applies, and you need at least three scores for a first index.

How does a 9-hole round count?
The differential for the nine holes played is combined with an expected value for the nine not played: (Handicap Index × 1.04 + 2.4) ÷ 2. That is why a 9-hole round needs a Handicap Index at the time it was played.

What is the adjusted gross score?
Your gross score with every hole capped at net double bogey, which is par plus handicap strokes plus 2. Your club's results list usually shows it.

Where do I find Course Rating and Slope?
On the course's scorecard, listed per tee. The calculator remembers every course you enter once.

Does it cost anything, do I need an account?
No to both. Your data stays in your browser. Export and import move it from laptop to phone.
